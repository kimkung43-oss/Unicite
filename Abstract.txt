Abstract

The Unicite Integrated Learning System (ILS) is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to enhance blended learning and provide a seamless learning experience for both teachers and students. 
It offers a range of features and functionalities that facilitate effective communication, collaboration, and resource management in an educational setting.

With Unicite ILS, teachers can create and manage courses, upload course materials, schedule classes, and conduct live online sessions with interactive tools such as screen sharing, whiteboard, and polls. Students can enroll in courses, access course materials, participate in discussions, submit assignments, and track their progress through the system.

The system incorporates modern technologies such as Progressive Web Applications (PWA) and USSD to ensure accessibility and usability across various devices and network conditions.
It integrates with external educational library APIs to provide access to a wide range of resources such as books, articles, and past papers. 
Additionally, Unicite ILS incorporates analytics and reporting functionalities to provide insights into learning outcomes, user engagement, and system performance.

The goal of Unicite ILS is to create an intuitive and user-friendly platform that empowers educators and learners to thrive in a digital learning environment.
 By leveraging advanced features, seamless integration, and comprehensive analytics, Unicite ILS aims to revolutionize the way education is delivered and experienced, ultimately enhancing the learning outcomes for all stakeholders.

Keywords: Integrated Learning System, Blended Learning, Communication, Collaboration, Resource Management, Progressive Web Application, USSD, Analytics
